The note explains interfaces and consequences of the formalization for a mathematical reader. It references the principal results of Delcourt–Postle's article and Postle's survey, without repeating their absorption development. The companion identifies exact formal declarations, source versions, build evidence and reproduction limits.

The five-directory structure follows the author's Nash–Williams Papers I–II release:

- `01_manuscript`: the note in both languages and formats.
- `02_validation`: [review summary](02_validation/REVIEW_SUMMARY.md), semantic, scope, bibliography, visual and mechanical evidence.
- `03_reproducibility`: [reconstruction guide](03_reproducibility/README.md).
- `04_integrity`: [hashes and package boundaries](04_integrity/README.md).
- `05_formalization`: the full bilingual companion.

The immutable [formal archive](../../publication/formal-freeze/formal-v1.0-5428ee9b772e.tar.gz) predates manuscript authoring. A document package, public release and Palomar registration are distinct artifacts. Use the complete repository for the relative links and verification tools.
